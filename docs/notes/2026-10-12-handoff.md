## What the game told us this time (details in `reference/` and the stories)

- **A city needs a road under it**; links are rebuilt from the roads on load. Moving a city lays one if missing.
- **A tower is two encounter records** (one per plane) cleared together; moving or editing it touches both.
- **Treasure code (`+14`) does not decide a lair's reward.** Spells, books and items are rolled when it is cleared;
  gold and mana are the record's own fields. The code's meaning is unknown.
- **Unit status:** 3 is a go-to order and always has a target; 4 is what a unit with no orders gets after a turn;
  1 is *guess:* sentry. A move or an owner change clears only a go-to order.
- **A melded node is just an owner byte**, and its guardians are cleared separately; in every save an owned node has
  them cleared. 37 owned nodes have no Guardian Spirit on their tile, and the game keeps such a node over End Turn.
- **The level byte is what the game shows on load, and it is worked out from experience again at End Turn**
  (experience is capped at 120 on a normal unit). So experience is the lasting field.
- **A city's size class (`+19`) is not a function of population** and is not recomputed; enchantment colour follows
  the caster's banner; race and owner changes are accepted (units in the city change hands, enchantments are cleared).
- Names are ASCII only (the game's font has no ü).

## How the real-game checks were run (worked every time)

1. Build the test save from the app's own code (`Mirror.SaveFile.Structures` and friends), from a copy of a played
   save, and diff it against its source: only the intended bytes may differ.
2. Back the target slot up to `~/.mirror/dev/DOSbox/backups/` first. Slots 1 and 2 are Kevin's (slot 2 is his played
   game, read-only), 3 to 8 are for test saves, 9 is the autosave.
3. `bash scripts/live_session.sh start` (Kevin runs it from the repo), then he loads the slot with **Load Game**.
4. Read the running game's memory over `http://127.0.0.1:8086/api/v1/memory/0x…/N` (wizards `0x328BA`, cities
   `0x6F980`, units `0x7E060` with the count at `0x34782`, nodes `0x85FE0`, towers `0x86610`, encounters `0x86660`,
   terrain `0x72630`) and run `python3 scripts/verify_loaded_save.py FILE`; checkpoint with
   `bash scripts/live_session.sh cp "name" "note"`.
5. Kevin cannot read map coordinates in the game, so test content goes next to a city of his and is described by
   direction and distance from it. Ask him for one End Turn after each load: several things only showed after it.
